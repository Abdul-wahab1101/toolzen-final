  1. Open the 20KB image compressor.

Frequently asked questions

Will every image reach exactly 20KB?

No. Compression results depend on image dimensions, content and format. The tool attempts to meet the target; always check the final file size.

Will the photo remain clear?

A 20KB limit is very small. Fine details may be lost, so check the output at normal viewing size.

Is the process private?

The image is processed locally in your browser for this tool.
