Rotate PDF pages online

Fix sideways or upside-down pages without changing the rest of your document. Choose a rotation angle and optionally limit it to selected pages.

Open Rotate PDF pages online ↗

How to use this tool

  1. Select the PDF file.
  2. Choose 90°, 180° or 270° rotation.
  3. Optionally select only the pages to rotate.
  4. Process and download the updated PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Select specific page thumbnails or enter page numbers where supported.

Will the page content be edited?

Rotation changes page orientation; it does not rewrite the text or images.

Are PDFs uploaded?

The rotation is performed locally in your browser.
